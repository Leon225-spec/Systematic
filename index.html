Built from real work experience

In many businesses, daily operations are still manual and disorganized.

Relying on memory

Staff rely on memory to find products. Warehouses are difficult to navigate, and workers waste time searching for items instead of working efficiently.

Slowed customer service

Lack of instant product knowledge slows everything down. Staff spend time asking others for help and trying to understand details while customers wait.

Lack of clear insights

At the same time, managers fly blind. They lack clear visibility into what products are selling most and what items customers are asking for regularly.

Introducing DispatchMate

DispatchMate is a smart operational assistant designed to help warehouse staff and shop workers perform their tasks faster and more accurately.

It allows staff to search products instantly, view item locations, understand product details, and fulfill customer orders without confusion. DispatchMate also provides simple insights into product trends and stock movement, helping businesses make better decisions.

Find items instantly

Search by product name or code and locate items quickly.

Understand products

View product details and specifications to assist customers confidently.

Know where items are

See clear warehouse locations to reduce time spent searching.

Track trends

Understand what products sell most and what customers ask for.
